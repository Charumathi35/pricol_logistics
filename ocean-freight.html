Overview

Pricol Logistics is a licensed Non-Vessel Operating Common Carrier (NVOCC) and freight forwarding company providing reliable ocean freight solutions across global trade routes. Through strategic partnerships with leading shipping lines and an extensive international network, we deliver flexible, reliable and cost-effective shipping solutions for import and export cargo.

Our difference

  • Strategic partnerships with leading global carriers
  • Coverage across 100+ countries
  • Competitive freight rates
  • Priority space allocation during peak seasons
  • Frequent sailing schedules and reliable transit times
  • WCA, MTO & GLA memberships
  • AEO & FIEO Accreditation
  • Real-time shipment tracking
  • Secure cargo handling and monitoring
