Overview

Pricol Logistics provides integrated warehousing, storage, distribution and third-party logistics (3PL) solutions that help businesses optimise inventory, streamline supply chain operations and improve operational efficiency. With strategically located warehouse facilities in Coimbatore, Chennai, Pune and Gurgaon, complemented by Free Trade Warehousing Zone (FTWZ) infrastructure, we deliver flexible warehousing, inventory management, distribution and value-added logistics solutions that enable businesses to scale operations, improve inventory visibility and enhance order fulfilment performance with lean management.

Our difference

  • Strategically located warehousing facilities across key logistics hubs
  • 4+ Connected DTA Warehouses
  • 1,00,000 Sq. Ft. FTWZ Warehouse Space
  • Flexible storage solutions for diverse business requirements
  • End-to-end fulfilment capabilities
  • Warehouse Management System (WMS)
  • Transport Management System (TMS)
  • Real-time inventory tracking and visibility
  • Just-in-time (JIT) and Kanban inventory management
  • On-demand trained manpower support
  • Single point of contact for seamless logistics coordination
